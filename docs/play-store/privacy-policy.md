# Privacy Policy — Shreeram Doodh Diary & Sweets

Published at **https://shreeram-dairy-milk.vercel.app/privacy** (source:
`apps/web/src/app/(public)/privacy/page.tsx`). That page is what Google Play links to — if you change
this text, change the page in the same commit so they never drift apart.

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

Shreeram Doodh Diary & Sweets ("we", "us") runs the **Shreeram Doodh Diary & Sweets** Android
app and the admin website at shreeram-dairy-milk.vercel.app. The app is used only by our own
staff — delivery workers and administrators — to manage daily milk deliveries, one-time
orders, and monthly bills. There is no public sign-up; accounts are created by our
administrator.

## Information we collect

**About staff who use the app (workers and administrators)**
- Name, email address and phone number, used to create and identify your account.
- Your sign-in session, stored securely on your phone so you stay signed in.
- The deliveries you record (customer, date, quantity, status, optional note, and the time
  your phone recorded it).

**About our customers (entered by our staff, not by the customers themselves)**
- Name, mobile number, optional email, delivery address, locality and landmark.
- Milk type, daily quantity, route, delivery start date, and any notes.
- Delivery records, one-time orders, monthly bills, adjustments and payments received
  (amount, method such as cash/UPI/bank transfer, and reference).

We do **not** collect location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access, device
advertising IDs, or browsing activity. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or
crash-reporting SDKs.

## How we use it

Only to run the dairy's delivery and billing operations: showing each worker their route,
recording deliveries, calculating and sharing monthly bills, tracking payments, and
producing business reports. We never sell this information or use it for advertising.

## Who can see it

- **Delivery workers** see only the customers assigned to them and the deliveries and
  orders they need to fulfil. Workers never see prices, bills or payments.
- **Administrators** see all business records.
- Access rules are enforced on the server (database row-level security), not just in the
  app.

We do not share personal information with third parties, except the service providers that
host the app's data on our behalf:
- **Supabase** — database and sign-in (data stored on servers in Seoul, South Korea).
- **Vercel** — hosts the admin website.

When an administrator shares a bill or report as a PDF, it is sent using your phone's own
share sheet (e.g. WhatsApp or email) — only to the recipient you choose.

## Storage on your phone

To work without network coverage, the app keeps a copy of your assigned customers and the
deliveries you recorded on the phone, and syncs them to our server when you're online. This
data is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

## Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in sessions are stored in the
phone's secure storage. Each account can only access what its role allows.

## Retention and deletion

- Staff accounts are kept while you work with us. Delivery and billing records are kept for
  as long as needed for accounting and legal purposes.
- **To delete your account or request deletion of your data**, follow the steps at
  https://shreeram-dairy-milk.vercel.app/delete-account or email us. Records we must keep for
  accounting (for example, bills already issued and payments received) may be retained in
  anonymised or restricted form.
- Customers can ask us to correct or delete their details the same way.

## Children

The app is for adult staff of the business and is not directed at children under 18.

## Changes

We'll update this page if our practices change and revise the "Last updated" date above.
